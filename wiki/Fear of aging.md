Fear of aging — the driving force behind many people's failure to transition from the look and behaviors of youth to those appropriate to an individual who is aging. Some people go to desperate and absurd lengths to approximate youthful appearance well into their middle age, sometimes with surgical interventions. 

## Madonna

Madonna's example shows that it's not fear of aging alone, but the culture's obsession with youth, that drives people toward becoming laggards in accepting their aging body. Madonna, who has had and used her power to change the way the culture viewed young women, appears to be powerless against this.

> Madonna introduced, flaunted and shattered culture’s deepest taboos, particularly around sexual agency for women, religion and identity. Even single motherhood. She was foundational to my understanding of how to be an unrepentant woman in the world. ... If Madonna can’t hold fast against the forces that demand that women stay appearing young, what chance do the rest of us have? ... In the end, we are all vain creatures desperate to hold on to, by any means possible, a shred of youth.

[Glynnis MacNicol](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/07/02/opinion/madonna-confessions-ii-aging-old.html).
