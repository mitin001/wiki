Reverse osmosis — a water filtration technique that can remove the most consequential contaminants found in the drinking tap water: [disinfection byproducts], [arsenic], and PFAS. In contrast, charcoal filtration can only remove disinfection byproducts but not arsenic or PFAS.

[disinfection byproducts]: https://ntp.niehs.nih.gov/research/assessments/cancer/completed/haa
[arsenic]: https://www.cancer.gov/about-cancer/causes-prevention/risk/substances/arsenic
