Blame the model — a trend among enterprise customers of OpenAI to blame their underwhelming return on investment in AI on the GPT model and its perceived inferiority to Anthropic's models. In reality, expectations in AI were massively inflated, and Anthropic's overtaking of OpenAI as the market leader in enterprise AI simply provided a convenient explanation they could use without undermining the narrative of AI being be-all and end-all or copping to the fact they've overinvested in a bubble. Meanwhile, the framing of Anthropic as the custodian of superior models propels it even further in its AI race against OpenAI and Google.
